## Featured project

### [CRM Analytics System for FMCG Companies](https://github.com/KennVanda/crm-fmcg-oop)
*Python · OOP · matplotlib · Google Colab · Nov 2026*

Course project, Fundamentals of Programming I, ITHB (team of 2, with Kenneth Mahinda Vanda)

- Co-built an AI-assisted Python CRM with 18 classes that apply all six OOP principles to model companies, customers, FMCG products, and transactions.
- Explained the OOP design in our demo: abstract classes, protected stock (encapsulation), and category-based pricing (polymorphism).
- Ran the live demo answering three business questions: loyal customers by share of wallet, top-k spenders, and each customer's favorite products.
- Presented the data findings from 289 simulated transactions (Rp36.7M): the top 5 spenders were all Regular customers outside the loyalty program, and 2 previously loyal customers had been inactive for 100+ days.
- Verified the system with 20 automated unit tests, including rejected purchases above stock or from the wrong company.
